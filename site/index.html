Serial bootloader utility · v4.7

Talk straight to the silicon.

esptool is the open-source Python utility that speaks the ROM bootloader protocol of every Espressif chip. Flash firmware, read memory, burn eFuses and sign images over one USB cable, with no IDE in the way.

$pip install esptool

The toolkit

Three tools ship in one pip install.

esptool.py

esptool

Flash, read, verify and erase. Convert ELF files into bootable images, merge binaries and query chip IDs, MAC addresses and flash parameters.

esptool.py write_flash 0x0 app.bin
espefuse.py

espefuse

Read and burn the one-time-programmable eFuses that hold MAC addresses, flash voltage, keys and security configuration.

espefuse.py --port /dev/ttyUSB0 summary eFuses only go one way. Measure twice.
espsecure.py

espsecure

Generate signing keys, sign images for Secure Boot and encrypt or decrypt data for Flash Encryption, all offline on your machine.

espsecure.py sign_data --version 2 --keyfile key.pem app.bin
Get started

Ten seconds to your first flash.

  • Python 3.7 or newer on Linux, macOS or Windows.
  • A USB cable that carries data, not just power. More boards are lost to charge-only cables than to bad firmware.
  • Serial port access. On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group.
  • Stuck at "Connecting..."? Hold BOOT, tap RESET, and try again.
